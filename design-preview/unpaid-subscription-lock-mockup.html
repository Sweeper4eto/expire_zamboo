Unpaid client — homepage only

When a business account is unpaid (payments required + current month not marked paid), the user can still open the app but all menus/features are blocked except the homepage.

Proposed behavior
  • Allowed: homepage (/app), logout, language (optional), support/contact (open question below).
  • Blocked: Expiry, Scan/Add, Document, Orders, Team, Settings — bottom nav taps do nothing / redirect home.
  • Message (EN): “Please check if your subscription is paid.”
  • Message (BG): „Моля, проверете дали абонаментът ви е платен.“
  • Change from today: currently unpaid users are blocked at login. This mockup lets them in, then locks the app.
